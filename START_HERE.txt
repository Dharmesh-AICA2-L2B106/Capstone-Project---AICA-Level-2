LIFE PLANNER
1. Extract the ZIP completely. Do not run inside the ZIP.
2. Double-click Run_Life_Planner.bat on Windows.
3. Requires Python 3 with Tkinter (included in the standard Windows installer).
   No pip packages, subscriptions or API keys required.
4. Add your real tasks and choose date/time, reminder lead and repeat pattern.
   Work: client follow-up, payroll review, meetings.
   Daughter / School: homework, activity day, uniform, school bag.
   Home / Personal: appointments, shopping, family tasks.
5. Tomorrow is the default view. Today and All active also show relevant tasks;
   check All active for overdue items. Completed tasks remain in Completed.
6. Complete a recurring task to create its next future occurrence automatically.
   Missed occurrences are skipped, not duplicated. Monthly dates are clamped to
   the last valid day; following months use that clamped date.
7. Reminders appear with a sound inside desktop popup windows. Keep this app
   open (minimized is fine), your computer awake, and your clock/time zone correct.
   There are no mobile, WhatsApp, email or closed-app notifications.
   Unfinished tasks repeat reminders every 30 minutes. Snooze delays 10 minutes.
   On reopening, overdue reminders resume. Run only one app window at a time.
8. Data persists in LOCALAPPDATA\MunazzaLifePlanner\planner.sqlite3 on Windows.
   CSV exports include all tasks for reference; CSV import is not included.
   For full recovery, close the app and copy planner.sqlite3 to a safe backup.
9. This is an offline planner, not an AI scheduling service. No school or work
   account is connected and no real reminders are configured until you add tasks.
