# 3.12 Input-VAT checks (FTA Decisions 13 and 17 of 2026) – plan

Prompt: input_vat service per SPEC 3.8 as a background job. Inputs: purchase lines, the supplier
verification register, and the employee-expense account mapping to the six categories. All dates,
periods and thresholds come from `input_vat.*` rules valid on the deduction date. Output exceptions
for the testing grid and the WP sheet "Input VAT". Tests against answer_key.csv.

## Data
* Migration 0007: input_vat_runs gets job_id, result, created_by, created_at (same as je_runs 0003).
* The supplier register is stored per engagement and replaced on each upload. CSV aliases:
  party_id/supplier_id, party_name/supplier_name, trn, verified_on,
  verification_ref/evidence_ref, bank_evidence_ref, risk_indicators (`;`-separated), explanation.
* Expense mapping per engagement: account_code -> category, where category must be one of
  `input_vat.employee_expense.categories`.

## Service `app/services/input_vat.py`
* Purchase line: vat_amount > 0, debit > 0, with a party_id. Gross = debit + vat_amount.
* Rule date: the deduction date (posting date, else entry date). With the TEST-ONLY
  `effective_date_override` (D4), each date is shifted by (rule effective date - override) before
  the rule lookup, so the same pending_verification versions apply to the 2025 test data.
* Checks (D40):
  1. `unverified_supplier`: deduction on or after the effective date, and the supplier is not in
     the register, has no verified_on, was verified after the deduction, or was verified more
     than `revalidate_months` before it. One exception per line.
  2. `spend_threshold`: rolling 12-month gross per supplier is above `spend_threshold`, the
     supplier has no bank_evidence_ref, and the crossing date is on or after the effective date.
     One exception per supplier (the crossing line), carrying the total.
  3. `risk_indicator_unexplained`: a supplier with risk indicators but no explanation, and
     deductions on or after the effective date. One exception per supplier.
  4. `employee_expense`: input-VAT lines in mapped accounts, listed by category for condition
     testing (whole period; `before_effective_date` is marked on each line).
  5. `staff_cost_unmapped`: an account not in the mapping where lines carry an expense_category,
     or the account name looks like staff cost (staff/employee/salar/wage) and has input VAT.
     One exception per account.
* Exemption values (10,000 / 100,000) are noted on exceptions as `below_exception_threshold`
  but never applied, because that is a Tax Partner judgement.
* Result: summary [{check, lines, value}], exceptions, employee_expense_by_category,
  conclusion, basis, rules_pending_verification, rule_versions_used, warnings.
* Job task `input_vat_check` (registered in TASK_MODULES). Streams projected Parquet columns.

## API
* `PUT /v1/engagements/{id}/supplier-register` (CSV upload) and `GET`
* `PUT /v1/engagements/{id}/expense-mapping` (JSON) and `GET`
* `POST /v1/input-vat-check` -> 202 {input_vat_run_id, job_id, status_url, result_url}
* `GET /v1/input-vat-check/{id}`
* `GET /v1/input-vat-check/{id}/exceptions?check&offset&limit`
* Writes need Manager/Partner/Staff membership; reads need membership.
