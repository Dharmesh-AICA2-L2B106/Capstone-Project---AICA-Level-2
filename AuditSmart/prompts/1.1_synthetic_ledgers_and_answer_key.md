# PROMPT – AI STUDIO | 1.1 Synthetic ledgers and answer key

Source: AuditSmart Build Guide v2.0, Stage 1. Copied verbatim; ' / ' marks line breaks in the original.

Implemented directly as data/make_gl.py instead of being run in AI Studio. Deviations are listed in docs/STAGE1_REPORT.md §Assumptions.

```
Write a Python script (seed 2025) producing three CSV files for a UAE trading company,
FY 1 Jan 2025 - 31 Dec 2025, and a second FY 2021-2022 extract for weekend-change tests.
1) dummy_gl.csv - 3,000 lines, multi-line balanced vouchers. Columns: line_id,
entry_date, posting_date, posting_time, voucher_no, account_code, account_name,
account_type, debit, credit, currency, narration, user_id, party_id, party_name, trn,
invoice_no, net_amount, vat_amount, vat_code (SR/ZR/EX/RC), expense_category.
Realistic UAE accounts (Emirates NBD, DEWA, rent Dubai, salaries WPS, staff transport,
staff accommodation, mobile, parking, VAT payable/receivable).
Include: 8 items larger than a typical MUS interval (top stratum); 40 credit-note lines;
company work week Sat/Sun off with Friday half day to 12:00.
Plant and record in answer_key.csv (line_id, error_type): 25 Sat/Sun postings,
10 Friday postings after 15:00, 15 public-holiday postings (from a provided list),
20 round sums (multiples of 10,000), 15 entries within 3 days of year-end by "FINMGR",
10 vague/blank narrations, 10 duplicate invoices, 12 output-VAT errors, 5 invalid TRNs,
15 purchases after 1 Oct 2025 from suppliers with no verification date,
3 suppliers whose rolling 12-month spend exceeds AED 375,000,
20 employee-expense lines in categories transport/food/accommodation/mobile/parking.
2) edge_cases.csv - 60 lines: 2 unbalanced vouchers, 5 unreadable dates, amounts with
commas and brackets, blank accounts, duplicate line_ids, 15-digit and 14-digit TRNs.
3) fy2021_2022.csv - 400 lines spanning 1 Dec 2021 - 31 Jan 2022 with postings on
Fridays and Sundays to test a Fri/Sat -> Sat/Sun weekend change on 1 Jan 2022.
```
